# SAT & ACT Research Notes (2026–2027 cycle)

Compiled September 2026 from College Board, ACT, Inc., and major prep providers (Kaplan, Princeton Review, CollegeWise). Used to design the question bank, practice modes, and marketing copy of this site.

## Digital SAT (current format, unchanged for 2026–27)

- **Format:** Fully digital since March 2024, taken in the College Board **Bluebook app**.
- **Length:** 2 hours 14 minutes of testing + 10-minute break between sections.
- **Structure:** 2 sections, each split into 2 timed modules (4 modules total). Timing is enforced **per module** — you cannot carry time forward.
- **Adaptive:** Multistage adaptive. Module 1 is a mixed-difficulty "router"; Module 2 difficulty mix depends on Module 1 performance. A harder Module 2 unlocks the full 800-per-section ceiling.
- **Sections:**
  - Reading & Writing: 54 questions, 64 min (2 × 32 min modules, 27 Qs each). Short passages, one question each. Domains: **Information and Ideas, Craft and Structure, Expression of Ideas, Standard English Conventions.**
  - Math: 44 questions, 70 min (2 × 35 min modules, 22 Qs each). ~75% multiple-choice, ~25% student-produced responses (grid-ins — type the answer, no choices). **Calculator allowed on all math questions.**
- **Scoring:** 400–1600 (200–800 per section). Adaptive scoring considers question difficulty, not just raw correct count.
- **SAT Math content domains (College Board weights):**
  - Algebra ~35% (13–15 Qs): linear equations/inequalities, linear functions, systems of two linear equations.
  - Advanced Math ~35% (13–15 Qs): equivalent expressions, nonlinear equations & systems, nonlinear functions (quadratic, exponential, polynomial).
  - Problem-Solving and Data Analysis ~15% (5–7 Qs): ratios/rates/percentages, one- and two-variable data, probability, samples & margin of error.
  - Geometry and Trigonometry ~15% (5–7 Qs): area/volume, lines/angles/triangles, right triangles & trig, circles.
- **Not tested on SAT Math:** logarithms, complex numbers, calculus, proofs, standard deviation computation.

## Enhanced ACT (live nationally since Sept 2025; School-Day rollout Spring 2026)

- **Core test (required):** English + Math + Reading only — **131 questions, ~2h 5m**.
  - English: 50 questions, 35 min (was 75/45). 6 shorter passages. ~60% grammar/conventions, ~40% rhetoric/content. Reporting: Production of Writing, Knowledge of Language, Conventions of Standard English.
  - Math: 45 questions, 50 min (was 60/60). **4 answer choices** instead of 5. Fewer advanced-topic questions; ~20% integrating essential skills (pre-high-school). Content otherwise unchanged.
  - Reading: 36 questions, 40 min (was 40/35). Shorter passages.
- **Science is now OPTIONAL:** 40 questions, 40 min. Does **not** count toward the 1–36 Composite (composite = English + Math + Reading only); feeds a separate STEM score.
- **Writing (essay) still optional:** 40 min, one essay.
- **More time per question** than the legacy ACT (+12–27% depending on section).
- **Scoring:** 1–36 per section + Composite (avg of English, Math, Reading).

## What students struggle with / search for (2026)

1. **Pacing under module timing** — the #1 complaint for both tests; students want timed practice that mirrors real modules.
2. **SAT Math is 70% Algebra + Advanced Math** — linear systems, quadratics, exponents, and function notation dominate.
3. **Percentages, ratios, and data interpretation** — small domain, high miss rate.
4. **Trig & circle equations** — small but feared; high tutoring demand.
5. **Grammar conventions** (both tests) — punctuation, verb agreement, concision; very coachable, fast score gains.
6. **ACT vs SAT choice** — students want guidance on which test fits them (speed vs. depth).
7. **Test anxiety & guessing strategy** — no guessing penalty on either test; never leave blanks.
8. **Score plateau 1200s–1300s (SAT) / mid-20s (ACT)** — where most tutoring inquiries come from.

## Implications for this site

- Practice presets mirror real modules: SAT Math 22 Qs / 35 min; ACT Math 45 Qs / 50 min; quick drills 10 Qs untimed.
- Question bank tagged by official domains so students can drill weakest areas.
- Marketing copy emphasizes: timed adaptive-style practice, domain analytics, 1-on-1 coaching for plateaus, and SAT-vs-ACT guidance.
- All questions on this site are **original**, written to the blueprints above — never copied from official tests.
